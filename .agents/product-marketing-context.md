## What It Is

A community voting tournament on Base where fans vote with ETH to determine which 48 of 211 national teams qualify for, and advance through, the Onchain World Cup bracket — completely independent of real FIFA match results.

**It is NOT:**
- A prediction market (you are not predicting real-world sports outcomes)
- Gambling (no house edge, no odds, no randomness)
- Related to real FIFA 2026 match results

**It IS:**
- A community support mechanism — you back your national team with ETH
- A tournament that runs parallel to FIFA World Cup 2026, with its own bracket and winner
- Financially meaningful — 90% of the prize pool is returned to supporters of qualifying teams

## How It Works

1. **Connect wallet** — email, Google, Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, or any Base-compatible wallet
2. **Choose a country** — 211 nations compete in qualification
3. **Vote with ETH** — linear bonding curve pricing; first vote costs 0.001 ETH, each subsequent vote for the same country increases by 0.0005 ETH
4. **Top 48 countries** by total vote count qualify and advance to group stages + knockout rounds
5. **Winners share 90%** of the unified prize pool, proportional to votes cast for qualifying countries

## Key Mechanics

- **Early-voter advantage:** Each country has its own independent vote count and price curve. Voting early for your country is always cheaper — rewards conviction over bandwagoning.
- **Multi-country strategy:** You can vote for multiple countries to spread risk across multiple potential qualifiers.
- **Gas fees:** Under $0.01 on Base — ETH spent is almost entirely the vote price.
- **Platform fee:** Up to 10%, deducted before prize distribution. Fee changes announced in advance.
- **Smart contracts:** Open-source, publicly verifiable on Basescan. All payouts are automatic — no manual intervention.

## Referral Program

**Mechanic:** Earn 1% of every ETH vote you refer — paid instantly and directly to your wallet when someone votes using your link. No claiming, no waiting.

**How it works:**
1. Connect wallet and go to your profile
2. Copy your referral link: `app.onchainworldcup.xyz/r/{your-address}`
3. Share it anywhere — social, DMs, Farcaster frame
4. When someone votes via your link, 1% of their vote amount is sent to your wallet on-chain, immediately

**Key facts:**
- 1% of vote amount (not prize pool) — e.g., vote of 0.001 ETH earns referrer 0.00001 ETH
- Deducted from platform fee — **the prize pool is not affected**
- Trustless: handled entirely by the smart contract (`WorldCupMatch.sol`)
- Referral link also works as a **Farcaster mini app frame** — people can vote directly from the cast
- Referral dashboard in profile shows total ETH earned + full activity log

**Messaging angle:** Sharing is financially meaningful. You don't just spread the word — you earn from it, on-chain, instantly.

## Competitive Positioning

Against prediction markets (Polymarket, etc.): We are not a prediction market. No odds. No betting on FIFA results. You are backing your team — and your team's fate is decided by the community, not on the pitch.

Against fantasy football: No team management, no per-player stats. Simpler — pick a country, vote, win if your country qualifies.
