What part of your product is magic or impressive?*
The two-phase dynamic pricing mechanism is the core innovation. In phase 1 (first 2 hours of a match), votes are priced linearly. In phase 2 (hours 2–24), pricing becomes exponential — heavily rewarding early conviction. Prize distribution is handled entirely by smart contracts with no human intervention. It's also a native Farcaster Mini App, so users can vote directly from their Farcaster feed without leaving the social experience.

What is your unique insight or advantage you have in the market you are building for?*
Three unfair advantages converge here.

First, timing. Most prediction apps wait for the tournament. The 2026 World Cup has 192 nations fighting for 48 spots across a qualification cycle that runs for some weeks before the first group game.

Second, domain knowledge. The founder is a football fan itself who watches matches across every confederation and knows the World Cup squads cold. This isn't a crypto dev guessing at what football fans want — it's someone who lives the sport building the product they wish existed.

Third, onchain credibility. After nearly 4 years writing, deploying, and auditing smart contracts at Talent Protocol, the founder knows exactly how to build onchain products that are safe and trustworthy — not just functional. That matters when users are putting real ETH on the line.
