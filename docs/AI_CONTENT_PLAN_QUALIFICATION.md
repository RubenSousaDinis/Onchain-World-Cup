This document guides the AI content generation tool (`/admin/content`) for the **Qualification Phase** of Onchain World Cup. **Content starts:** March 2, 2026 (pending user feedback collection)
**Qualification launch:** TBD — flexible, targeting sometime in April
**Qualification closes:** May 31, 2026 (hard deadline)
**Content window:** March 2 through end of qualification

> **Note:** Qualification launch date is flexible while user feedback is being collected. The content calendar below uses a **T-minus format** relative to launch. The qualification can run as long as needed up to May 31.

---

## What Is the Qualification Phase?

Unlike traditional tournaments, Onchain World Cup's qualification phase is fully community-driven:

- Users vote for countries using ETH on the Base network
- Linear pricing: first vote costs 0.001 ETH, each subsequent vote for that country costs 0.0005 ETH more
- The **top 48 countries by vote count** qualify for the tournament
- All voters who backed a qualifying country **share the prize pool** proportionally by their vote count
- Voting for multiple countries is a valid strategy

Key message: **Your votes shape the tournament. Your early votes are cheaper and more powerful.**

## Content Themes by Phase

### Phase 0 — Awareness & Education (Mar 2 → T-3 weeks)
*Before the final countdown. Goal: explain the concept, build curiosity, grow audience.*

- What is the qualification phase and why it's unique
- How ETH voting works (linear pricing explained simply)
- Why early votes matter more
- Explainers: which countries are favourites, dark horses, underdogs
- Football-forward content with onchain hooks (not the other way around)
- Use this phase to collect engagement and iterate messaging based on feedback

### Phase 1 — Hype & Countdown (T-3 weeks → launch)
*Final push to launch. Goal: urgency, anticipation, community building.*

- "X days until qualification opens" countdown content
- Strategy guides: how to maximise your prize pool share
- Spotlight on specific countries with interesting angles
- Community prediction content (who do YOU think will make it?)
- Why Base network is the home for this

### Phase 2 — Live Qualification (Launch → T+last week)
*During voting. Goal: real-time engagement, FOMO, participation.*

- Weekly leaderboard recaps (which countries are leading, surprising movers)
- Strategy content as pricing evolves (expensive countries = why to look at alternatives)
- Country spotlight posts tied to real-world football news
- "Still time to vote" urgency content
- User highlight / success stories

### Phase 3 — Final Push (Last week → May 31)
*Final week before close. Goal: last burst of votes, excitement about results.*

- Countdown to close: "X days left"
- Bubble countries (currently just inside/outside top 48) — create drama
- Price snapshot posts (some countries now expensive, opportunities in others)
- Preview of the tournament bracket that will follow
- What happens after qualification closes (finalization, claiming, tournament draw)

## AI Content Generation Instructions

When using the content generator at `/admin/content`, follow these guidelines:

### Blog Posts

**Tone:** Passionate about football first, onchain second. Accessible to non-crypto readers.

**Structure:**
- Hook: start with the football/human angle, not the crypto mechanics
- Explain the onchain element as a feature that makes it better, not as the main point
- Use concrete numbers (0.001 ETH, top 48)
- End with a clear CTA to vote or share

**Prompt guidance examples:**
- For explainer posts: *"Write for someone who loves football but has never used crypto. Explain [topic] simply, with football analogies."*
- For strategy posts: *"Write for someone who has voted once and wants to maximise their returns. Be specific about numbers."*
- For country spotlights: *"Focus on the football story first, then connect to qualification mechanics."*
- For live updates: *"Use real energy. This is happening now. Be a reporter, not a marketer."*

### Twitter Threads

**Tone:** Direct, punchy, high energy. Football fan on Twitter voice.

**Rules:**
- Tweet 1 must be a standalone hook — reads well even without the thread
- Use numbers and specifics (not "a lot of ETH", but "0.001 ETH")
- Max 1 emoji per tweet, used sparingly
- Last tweet always has a CTA with the app link
- Threads should be 7-10 tweets

**Prompt guidance examples:**
- For announcement threads: *"Write like breaking news. Short sentences. High stakes."*
- For education threads: *"One concept per tweet. Build understanding step by step."*
- For leaderboard threads: *"Format it like a sports commentary. React to the numbers."*

---

## Content Don'ts

- Never call it "Crypto World Cup" — always **Onchain World Cup**
- Don't lead with "blockchain" or "Web3" in headlines — lead with football/stakes
- Don't use jargon like "DeFi", "gas fees", "smart contracts" in consumer-facing copy without explaining first
- Don't make price predictions about ETH
- Don't compare to gambling — this is voting and prize pools
- Don't over-promise returns — describe the mechanics accurately

## Key Messages to Reinforce

1. **The community decides the tournament** — not FIFA, not the platform
2. **Early votes = more power and lower cost** — act now
3. **You can vote for multiple countries** — spread your strategy
4. **Fully onchain = fully transparent** — no one can rig it
5. **Base network = fast and cheap** — low friction to participate
