# User Acquisition Playbook — Pre-Launch

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Scope:** Distribution strategy for the 9 days before mainnet launch (April 6–14)
**Goal:** 1 ETH in the prize pool on launch day (≈200–500 voters)
**Problem:** April 4 posts had near-zero reach (0 Farcaster likes, 82 Twitter views) because content was posted only to main feeds, not to the channels where the target audience actually lives.

---

## Context

- **Founder reach:** 600 Farcaster followers (@rubendinis), 90 Twitter followers (@RubenDinis12)
- **Brand reach:** minimal — new account
- **Primary channel:** Farcaster (crypto-native, Base-aligned audience)
- **Root cause of low reach:** Posts went to personal main feed only. No channel posts, no direct outreach, no ecosystem amplification.
- **Content quality is not the problem.** The April 4 posts (builder post + teaser video) are good. Distribution is the gap.

---

## Strategy: Three Parallel Tracks

### Track 1 — Farcaster Channel Distribution
**When:** Starting April 7 (tomorrow)
**Effort:** Low
**Expected impact:** 5–10x reach vs. main feed posts

Post existing content (builder post, teaser video, how-it-works) directly into Farcaster channels:
- `/base` — primary target, thousands of active Base ecosystem users
- `/onchain` — onchain-native audience
- `/soccer` — football fans who are crypto-adjacent

Rules for channel posts:
- Reply to every comment within the first 2 hours — Farcaster's algorithm rewards early conversation
- Don't just cross-post the same text verbatim — add a channel-appropriate one-liner at the top (e.g. "Built on Base, launching April 14 👇" for /base)
- Post the mini-app frame in the channel post body, not just a link

**Content to post in channels (in order):**
1. April 7: Builder post + teaser video → `/base` and `/onchain`
2. April 8: How-it-works post → `/base`
3. April 10: "Smaller nation can beat Brazil" angle → `/soccer`
4. April 13: 48-hours countdown → `/base` and `/onchain`
5. April 14: Launch post → `/base`, `/onchain`, `/soccer`

---

### Track 2 — Country Community Activation
**When:** April 7–13
**Effort:** Medium
**Expected impact:** Each activated community = 20–100 new voters with strong conversion

The product's core mechanic — fan votes decide outcomes — maps directly onto national football pride. This is the most native growth lever. A message from a Portuguese crypto influencer saying "Portugal needs your vote" converts far better than any broadcast post.

**Target countries (8–10):**
Brazil, Portugal, Argentina, Nigeria, India, Mexico, Japan, Senegal

These are chosen for: (1) large diaspora in crypto, (2) passionate football cultures, (3) active Farcaster/Base communities.

**Outreach approach:**
- Find 1–2 accounts per country with real Farcaster or Twitter followings
- DM directly — not a pitch, a human message. Example:
  > "Hey — I built something you might care about. It's a community World Cup on Base where fans vote to decide which countries qualify. Portugal currently has zero votes. Launches April 14. Thought you'd find it interesting."
- Goal: 3–5 responses, at least 2 who post about it before or on launch day

**How to find targets:**
- Search Farcaster for country names + "Base" or "ETH"
- Look at followers of @base who have country flags in their bios
- Twitter: search "[country] crypto" or "[country] Base"

---

### Track 3 — Base Ecosystem Amplification
**When:** April 7–10 (needs lead time before launch)
**Effort:** Medium–High
**Expected impact:** One @base recast = hundreds of new visitors

The Base team actively supports ecosystem projects. One mention from @base or Jesse Pollak has more reach than 1,000 personal posts.

**Actions:**
1. DM Jesse Pollak on Farcaster — short, human, direct:
   > "Hey Jesse — I've been building Onchain World Cup on Base for the last few months. It's a community tournament where football fans vote with ETH to decide which 48 of 211 countries qualify for the bracket. Launches April 14. Would mean a lot if you'd check it out."
2. Tag @base in the April 7 channel post in /base (natural, not forced)
3. Check if there's a Base ecosystem submission form or grants program — apply before April 14
4. Look at Base's recent ecosystem spotlights for the right contact if Jesse doesn't respond
